Privacy Policy

Citra Studio ("we," "us," or "our") provides this Privacy Policy to explain how Simple Mirror - Camera Mirror (the "App") handles information.

Developer
Citra Studio
Bundle ID
com.citrastudio.Mirror
Last updated
October 28, 2026

1. How the App works

The App displays the camera feed on an iPhone or iPad for use as a mirror. Its mirror features work without an internet connection. No account registration, in-app purchase, or subscription is required.

Camera frames are processed on the device and are not sent to our servers. The App does not automatically save photos or videos and does not provide a photo or video capture/export feature.

2. Camera access

The App uses the camera only to provide its mirror features. Camera access requires your permission through iOS. You can change this permission later in iOS Settings. Denying camera permission does not affect other functions of your device, but the App's mirror features will not be available.

3. Information collection and sharing

The App does not transmit or collect user data for us as “collection” is defined for Apple's App Privacy disclosures. We do not share or sell personal information, camera frames, paused images, or usage information to third parties.

The App contains no advertising SDK, third-party analytics SDK, or tracking SDK. We do not operate App user accounts or user-specific server storage.

4. Data stored on your device

Paused images

A paused image is held only in memory while the App process is running. It is not persistently saved to Photos or files and is discarded when the App process ends.

Settings

Your selected video quality and interpolation mode are stored locally in UserDefaults so they can be restored on a later launch. These settings are not sent to our servers.

Deleting the App normally deletes the local data managed by the App. Data management performed by the device, iOS, or backups remains subject to Apple's behavior and your settings.

5. External links

The App can open Apple App Store pages only after you explicitly select an external link. An internet connection may be required when opening those pages.

Data processing after you leave the App is governed by the terms and privacy policies of Apple or the applicable destination service. Such processing is separate from data collection by the App.

6. Deleting data

Because we do not maintain user accounts or server storage for camera images or user-specific App data, there is no user data for us to delete on our systems. Locally stored settings are normally removed when the App is deleted.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when laws, App features, or our information practices change. We will identify material changes on this page and update the “Last updated” date.
