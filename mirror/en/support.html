Simple Mirror - Camera Mirror is a straightforward mirror app with zoom, freeze, brightness, and video quality controls.

Requirements
iOS 15 or later
Devices
iPhone and iPad
Price
Free, with no ads
Account
Not required

Getting started

  1. Allow camera access on first launch to display the front camera.
  2. Tap the video once to show or hide the toolbar.
  3. Double-tap the video to freeze or resume it.
  4. Pinch to zoom in or out.
  5. While frozen, zoom the image further and drag it to adjust the visible area.

Controls

  • Switch camera: Switch between the front and rear cameras.
  • Mirror: Flip the displayed video horizontally.
  • Zoom: Adjust magnification with a pinch gesture or slider.
  • Freeze: Freeze the current frame or resume live video.
  • Display brightness: Adjust the device screen brightness from the App.
  • Video brightness: Adjust only the brightness of the camera video.
  • Screen light: Show a white border around the screen while using the front camera.
  • Quality: Choose from Auto, 1080p, 720p, 480p, 240p, 120p, 60p, and 30p-equivalent modes.
  • Interpolation: Choose Smooth or Pixel display when enlarging the video.

Frequently asked questions

Q. Why is the camera not displayed?
A. Check that camera access for Mirror is allowed in your iPhone or iPad Settings.
Q. Does Mirror save video or images?
A. No. Mirror does not provide a feature to save photos or videos.
Q. Is an internet connection required?
A. The mirror features work offline. A connection may be required when you open an external page such as the App Store.
Q. Is a frozen image retained?
A. A frozen image is held temporarily in App memory and is discarded when the App process ends.
Q. Are video quality settings saved?
A. Yes. Your selected quality and interpolation mode are stored on the device and restored on the next launch.
Q. Will my display brightness be restored?
A. When you leave the App normally, Mirror is designed to restore the display brightness it changed. Restoration cannot be guaranteed after an abnormal termination; in that case, adjust brightness in iOS Control Center.
