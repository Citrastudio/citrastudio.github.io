Basic Math Training

A focused practice app for building speed and accuracy in essential arithmetic.

Key features

  • Focused practice for the four basic operations
  • Elementary and junior-high problem sets
  • Custom problems with adjustable digit counts and negative numbers
  • No-miss mode, which ends after one mistake
  • A review list for problems that need more practice
  • Progress tracking and five achievement levels
  • Japanese and English, with dark and light themes
