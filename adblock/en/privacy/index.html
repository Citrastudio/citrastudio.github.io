Privacy Policy

Citra Studio ("we," "us," or "our") establishes this Privacy Policy to explain how information is handled in Ad Blocker & PiP Assistant (the "App").

1. Information We Collect

We do not operate our own servers to collect personal information, browsing history, or usage data through the App. The App has no user registration or login feature and does not use our own advertising or analytics SDKs.

Ad-blocking statistics, settings, per-site exclusions, and free-trial information required for the App are processed and stored on your device. We do not independently collect this information.

This describes automatic data collection by the App. If you voluntarily send us a support email, the handling of that information is described in Section 8.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not send or collect the following information through our own servers:

  • Your browsing history or a list of pages you visit
  • Information entered on web pages
  • Cookies, form contents, passwords, or other authentication information
  • Information that directly identifies you, such as your name, address, telephone number, or email address
  • Ad-blocking statistics, App settings, or per-site exclusions

Debug diagnostic features used during development are not included in the Release version distributed through the App Store.

3. Why Safari Extensions Access Web Pages

The App's Safari extensions use permission to access the page you are viewing to block or reduce ad elements, hide dynamic ads, assist video playback, and provide Picture-in-Picture (PiP), playback speed, and skip controls.

This access is used to provide features on your device. It is not used to send browsing history, page input, cookies, form contents, or passwords to our servers.

4. On-Device Storage

Ad-blocking statistics, App settings, per-site exclusions, and part of the state required to verify the license are stored on your device or in the App Group container shared by the App and its Safari extensions. Reference information such as the free-trial start date is stored in the device Keychain, while part of the free-trial and license-verification state is shared through the App Group container.

This information is used on your device to provide the App's features and is not sent to our own servers. Information in the Keychain may remain on the device after the App is deleted because of operating-system behavior.

5. App Store and StoreKit

The App uses Apple's StoreKit for the One-Time Purchase. Purchases, payments, purchase-status verification, and restoration are processed through Apple's systems.

Apple's Privacy Policy and applicable terms govern Apple's handling of information. We do not receive payment information such as your credit card number.

6. Third-Party Services

The App does not include our own advertising or analytics SDKs. It uses Apple's App Store and StoreKit as third-party services for in-app purchases.

Websites you visit in Safari may handle cookies, access information, or other data independently of the App. Please review each website's privacy policy for information about its practices.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy in response to changes in laws, services, or App features. An updated Policy takes effect when it is posted on this page.

8. Contact Us

For questions about this Policy or the App, contact us at the email address below.

We use the email address and message content you send us only to answer questions, investigate issues, and provide support. We do not use this information for advertising or marketing.
