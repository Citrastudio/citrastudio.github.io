Ad Blocker & PiP Assistant

Ad Blocker & PiP Assistant helps block or reduce web ads in Safari and provides convenient video playback controls.

Setup

To use the app, enable its Safari extensions in your device settings. Ad blocking and video playback assistance will not work while the Safari extensions are disabled.

Main Features

  • Blocking ad network requests
  • Hiding dynamic ad elements
  • Video ad countermeasures
  • Enhanced ad-blocking features
  • Picture-in-Picture (PiP) playback assistance
  • Playback speed adjustment from 0.1× to 5.0×
  • Play and pause controls
  • Skip backward and forward by a selected number of seconds
  • A collapsible and expandable floating controller
  • One-row or two-row layouts, button size, opacity, and other customizations
  • Per-site ad-blocking exclusions

How the App Works

Ad blocking and video playback assistance depend on each website's structure and delivery method. Some ads or video features may not work as expected because of website changes, delivery methods, or content restrictions. We plan to continue improving and expanding compatibility.

If the video controller does not appear or its buttons do not respond, refreshing the Safari page may resolve the issue.
