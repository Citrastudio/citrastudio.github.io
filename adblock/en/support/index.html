Ad Blocker & PiP Helper

Ad Blocker & PiP Helper helps block or reduce web ads in Safari and provides convenient video playback controls.

Setup

To use the app, enable its Safari extensions in your device settings. Ad blocking and video playback assistance will not work while the Safari extensions are disabled.

Main Features

  • Blocking ad network requests
  • Hiding dynamic ad elements
  • Video ad countermeasures
  • Enhanced ad-blocking features
  • Picture-in-Picture (PiP) playback assistance
  • Playback speed adjustment from 0.1× to 5.0×
  • Play and pause controls
  • Skip backward and forward by a selected number of seconds
  • A collapsible and expandable floating controller
  • One-row or two-row layouts, button size, opacity, and other customizations
  • Per-site ad-blocking exclusions
  • Hiding supported standard videos, Shorts cards, and channel cards that match registered YouTube channels
  • YouTube keyword filtering for selected fields: title, channel name, and description
  • Hiding Google search results that match registered domains or title keywords

YouTube Hiding and Keyword Filtering

Register a channel from a channel or video URL to hide supported standard video and Shorts list cards from Safari YouTube search results, the home feed, related videos, and other supported lists. Matching channel cards in search results are also supported. Remove the relevant rule to restore hidden items.

You can choose whether keyword filtering checks titles, channel names, descriptions, or a combination of them. When channel-name matching is enabled, matching channel cards in search results are also supported. Descriptions can be checked only when YouTube includes them in a list card.

The App tries to close gaps left by hidden Shorts cards where the page structure allows it, but YouTube's layout may prevent every gap from being removed. This feature does not automatically skip videos in the continuous Shorts player and does not hide the video player itself.

Website and Search Result Hiding

On supported standard Google web search pages, the App can hide results whose destination matches a registered domain or whose title matches a registered keyword. It does not remove or alter the content of the destination website itself.

How the App Works

Ad blocking, video playback assistance, YouTube hiding, and search-result filtering depend on each website's structure and delivery method. Some features may not work as expected because of website changes, delivery methods, or content restrictions. We plan to continue improving compatibility.

If the video controller does not appear or its buttons do not respond, refreshing the Safari page may resolve the issue.
